    How it was tested, and its limits
    
    
    
      
    
        
    The rule, fixed before testing
    
        
      
        
      
      
      
      
        
      Limits
      
        
        
          
      • Only 11 attacks hit the water process, so the true catch rate could be as low as about 70% (the 95% lower bound for 11 of 11 is 71.5%).
        • 
          
      • One lab testbed. HAI's water attacks change or replay valve and pump commands; other attack styles are untested.
        • 
          
      • Network traffic is not part of the check.
        • 
          
      • HAI 22.04 labels every attack for the whole plant; water-process attacks were matched to HAI's attack tables. Two table entries (A301, A305) contradict themselves and are left out of every count.
        • 
          
      • HAI 23.05's test-2 labels record times only to the minute; labels were aligned by row with a check that every row falls in the same minute.
